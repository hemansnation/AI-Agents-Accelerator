Billing and Account Information

Billing Procedures:
- Monthly billing occurs on the 1st of each month
- Annual subscriptions receive a 20% discount
- Invoices are sent via email within 24 hours of payment
- Payment methods can be updated in account settings

Refund Policy:
- Full refunds available within 30 days of purchase
- Partial refunds available after 30 days based on usage
- Refund processing takes 5-10 business days
- Contact customer support for refund requests

Account Management:
- Users can upgrade or downgrade plans at any time
- Prorated charges apply to plan changes
- Account data is retained for 90 days after cancellation
- Family plans allow up to 5 users

Subscription Tiers:
- Basic: $9.99/month - Essential features
- Pro: $24.99/month - Advanced features + priority support
- Enterprise: Custom pricing - Full feature access + dedicated support
